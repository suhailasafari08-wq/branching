Made for the love of tea.

Hira Tea Glass Store is a small celebration of beautiful glassware and the everyday moments that bring people together.

A little beauty in everyday life.

Tea has a way of making people feel welcome. It brings family around the table, gives friends a reason to stay a little longer, and turns an ordinary pause into a moment to enjoy.

We built Hira around that feeling. The collection brings together familiar tea glass shapes, pretty details and modern designs that look lovely at home.

Our aim is simple: make it easier to find glassware that feels right for your table and your way of enjoying tea.
